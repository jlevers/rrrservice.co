Tax is charged on the full selling price, not the markup. Where you already paid tax at purchase, Washington's taxable amount for tax paid at source deduction is what keeps you from paying twice — so the tax you finally remit does work out to roughly the tax on your markup, but that happens on the excise return, not on the customer's invoice.

Labor isn't in this calculator. In Washington, repair labor is generally taxable too, so the tax on the finished invoice will be higher than the figure above. Confirm the rate for the job site — Washington sources sales where the customer takes delivery, so a mobile job carries that location's rate, not your shop's.
